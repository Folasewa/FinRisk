SANCTIONS & HIGH-RISK JURISDICTION REFERENCE

HIGH-RISK JURISDICTIONS (FATF Grey/Black List - 2025)
The following countries are subject to enhanced monitoring:
- Myanmar, North Korea, Iran, Syria
- Russia (sectoral sanctions apply)
- Belarus (enhanced restrictions)

SECTOR-SPECIFIC SANCTIONS
Energy sector: Restrictions apply to Russian energy companies and 
entities providing goods/services to Russian energy projects.

Defense sector: Arms embargo applies to multiple jurisdictions. 
Dual-use goods require export license review.

COMMON RED FLAGS FOR SANCTIONS EVASION
- Use of shell companies in low-transparency jurisdictions
- Rapid movement of funds through multiple accounts
- Transactions inconsistent with business profile
- Involvement of third-party intermediaries without clear purpose

WATCHLIST SCREENING FREQUENCY
- Onboarding: Full screening required
- Ongoing: Automated daily screening against updated lists
- Event-triggered: Re-screen on any material change in ownership
