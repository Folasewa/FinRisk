BASEL III CREDIT RISK GUIDELINES

1. CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS
Banks must hold a minimum Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio of 4.5% of 
risk-weighted assets. The capital conservation buffer adds another 2.5%, 
bringing the effective minimum to 7%.

2. CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT STANDARDS
All corporate exposures must be assessed using either the Standardised 
Approach or the Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) approach. IRB requires 
estimating Probability of Default (PD), Loss Given Default (LGD), 
and Exposure at Default (EAD).

3. LEVERAGE RATIO
Banks must maintain a minimum leverage ratio of 3%, calculated as Tier 1 
capital divided by total exposure.

4. COUNTERPARTY RISK
For derivatives and securities financing, banks must calculate Credit 
Valuation Adjustment (CVA) to account for counterparty default risk.

5. STRESS TESTING
All institutions with assets over €30 billion must conduct annual stress 
tests simulating adverse macroeconomic scenarios.
