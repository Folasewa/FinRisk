SAMPLE CORPORATE CREDIT REPORT - REFERENCE TEMPLATE

COMPANY: Meridian Manufacturing GmbH
DATE: January 2025
ANALYST: Credit Risk Division

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Meridian Manufacturing presents moderate credit risk. Revenue has grown 
steadily at 8% CAGR over 3 years, however debt-to-equity ratio of 2.1x 
is above sector average of 1.4x. Recommend conditional approval with 
enhanced monitoring.

CREDIT SCORE: 64/100
RATING: BBB

KEY FINANCIAL METRICS
- Revenue: €142M (FY2024)
- EBITDA Margin: 14.2%
- Net Debt: €89M
- Interest Coverage Ratio: 3.2x
- Current Ratio: 1.4x

RISK FACTORS
- High leverage relative to peers
- Single customer concentration (top client = 31% of revenue)
- Exposure to raw material price volatility

MITIGATING FACTORS
- Long-standing banking relationship (7 years)
- Diversified geographic presence
- Strong management track record

RECOMMENDATION: CONDITIONAL APPROVE
Approve credit facility of €20M subject to quarterly covenant monitoring.
