INTERNAL AML & KYC POLICY REFERENCE

1. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
All corporate clients must undergo standard CDD including:
- Verification of legal entity and registration documents
- Identification of Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) holding >25% stake
- Assessment of business nature and expected transaction patterns

2. ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD)
EDD is triggered when:
- Client is from a FATF high-risk jurisdiction
- PEP (Politically Exposed Person) involvement is detected
- Transaction patterns are inconsistent with stated business purpose
- Adverse media screening returns negative results

3. POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEP)
Any individual holding or having held a prominent public function within 
the last 12 months is classified as PEP. This includes heads of state, 
senior politicians, senior executives of state-owned enterprises, and 
their immediate family members.

4. SANCTIONS SCREENING
All clients must be screened against:
- UN Consolidated Sanctions List
- EU Financial Sanctions List
- OFAC SDN List (US)
- UK HM Treasury Sanctions List

5. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING
Any transaction or pattern that cannot be explained must be reported 
via SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) within 24 hours of detection.
